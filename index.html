What a job looks like, start to finish

01 · Outreach

Chase clients without losing the thread

  • Contacts with a status, a full history and follow-up reminders.
  • Email templates with variables, edited and previewed on one screen.
  • Batch sending with your own daily cap, so you don't burn your mailbox.
  • Mail leaves your account over SMTP — the client sees your address, not a middleman's.
02 · The job

The assignment, with everything it carries

  • Language pair, source word count and rate — or hours, for work billed by time.
  • Import the analysis from Trados, memoQ or Wordfast and price each match band.
  • Deliverables attached to the job, sent from the job itself.
  • A glossary per client, shared across every project you do for them.
03 · The invoice

An invoice that knows where you're billing from

  • VAT and withholding under the name they actually have where you invoice — IRPF in Spain, ISR in Mexico.
  • 75 countries with their own tax profile, each with its own identifier and wording.
  • In Spain, an invoice ledger where each entry is chained by hash to the one before it, and corrective invoices on their own series.
  • Its own PDF, ready to send by email from the same screen.
  • And it warns you when your country requires the invoice to be filed with the tax authority: already the case in twelve of the countries it checks, and in Spain from July 2027 under Veri*Factu.
04 · Getting paid

Get paid, and know how it is going

  • A Stripe payment link inside the invoice itself.
  • Staged reminders that follow how late the payment is.
  • Recurring billing for retainer clients — always confirmed by you, never issued behind your back.
  • Metrics: collected per month and per client, average rate, average days to payment.
